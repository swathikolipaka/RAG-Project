Water freezes at 0 degrees Celsius under standard atmospheric pressure. This
temperature is also known as the freezing point of water and is used as one
of the two defining points of the Celsius temperature scale, the other being
the boiling point of water at 100 degrees Celsius. The freezing point can
shift slightly with changes in pressure or the presence of dissolved
substances, a phenomenon known as freezing point depression.
